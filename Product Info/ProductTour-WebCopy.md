# Joule Studio Product Tour — Web Copy

---

## Scenario 1: Define Your Intent

**Copy block 1**
Describe a business challenge, such as identifying orders impacted by new tariffs.

**Copy block 2**
Joule Studio enriches your prompt by grounding it in your enterprise data, processes, and governance controls.

**Copy block 3**
Joule Studio asks follow-up questions to refine requirements, identify the appropriate APIs, and validate your business logic.

**Copy block 4**
Based on the information gathered, Joule Studio produces a product requirements document (PRD) that captures the problem, personas, and user stories.

**Copy block 5**
Throughout the process, your SAP landscape and business process shape the solution architecture.

**Copy block 6**
Ask Joule Studio to adjust and refine the PRD until it fully maps to your business challenge.

## Scenario 2: Build the Solution

**Copy block 1**
Use Joule Studio to translate the PRD into a technical specification with clear implementation instructions for how AI will write the code.

**Copy block 2**
Review the code generated by Joule Studio. It's built to align with SAP frameworks out of the box.

**Copy block 3**
Iterate directly in the web-based editor or continue development in Claude Code and your preferred IDE, such as Visual Studio Code.
